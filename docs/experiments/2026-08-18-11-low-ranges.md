# EXP-11 — are ranges 0-3 sensitive rows, or genuinely broken?

- **Date:** 2026-08-18
- **Unit:** bench unit #1
- **Build:** `make guest-coldtrace`, build stamp `Aug 17 2026 23:26:37`
- **Status:** **PARTIALLY REFUTED** — the hypothesis fails for ranges 0/1/2 and
  survives, weakly, for range 3

## 1. Problem

`scope_cal.c` files ranges 0-3 as `SCOPE_CAL_NONE`: "both channels rail at
every amplitude, a property of the range table, purpose unknown". Every
measurement behind that verdict was taken at **2000 mVpp**.

Stlkv measured 0.4-8 mV/count rows on his unit that we have never reached, and
`CLAUDE.md` already carried the note that the sensitive half of the ladder was
not reachable in the frontend state we held. If ranges 0-3 are those rows, then
2 Vpp is 250 to 5000 counts into a 255-count ADC and railing is the *correct*
response to a signal a hundred times too large — meaning four working ranges
were filed as broken because they were only ever hit with a sledgehammer.

## 2. Hypothesis

Ranges 0-3 are high-gain taps that rail because the drive was far too large.

- **If true:** at small drive they produce a usable span that tracks amplitude,
  and the implied mV/count falls well below range 4's 14.08 / 9.49.
- **If false:** they stay pinned no matter how small the drive gets — including
  with no drive at all, which is the decisive case.

## 3. Procedure

Per range 0-5, both channels:

```
fpga scope range r 1 ; fpga scope range r 2
siggen OFF                    <- centre on a QUIET input
fpga scope center ch1 r ; fpga scope center ch2 r
record quiet span / mean
CH1 triangle 250 Hz, CH2 square 400 Hz
amplitudes 20 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 mVpp commanded
median span, mean and railed-sample count over 3 reads each
```

**Two method fixes over the EXP-08 pass**, either of which could have produced
the original verdict on its own:

1. **Centre with the input quiet.** EXP-08 centred with 2 Vpp applied. On a
   sensitive range that is centring a railing input, and the binary search
   cannot converge — so part of "centring failed" was self-inflicted.
2. **Regress against the REPORTED amplitude, not the commanded one.** This
   generator under-delivers by a roughly constant ~9 mV:

   | commanded | 20 | 50 | 100 | 200 | 500 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | reported | 12 | 38 | 90 | 194 | 491 |

   Negligible at 2 Vpp, but 40% of the signal at 20 mVpp. Using commanded
   amplitudes down here would manufacture a gain error out of nothing.

Railed samples are counted per window (`<=1` or `>=254`) and points with ≥50
railed samples are dropped from the fit — a clipped window carries no gain
information.

## 4. Control

| control | expected | measured | passed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| anchor: range 6, 2000 mVpp | ~49 / 51 counts (EXP-09) | **50.0 / 51.0** | ✓ |
| range 4 gain, small-amplitude fit | 14.08 / 9.49 known | 10.41 / 8.40 | ⚠ 26% / 11% |
| range 5 gain, small-amplitude fit | 21.83 / 20.96 known | 27.42 / 22.87 | ⚠ 26% / 9% |
| quiet floor at every range | a few counts | 0.0-5.0 | ✓ |

The anchor passes exactly, so this is the same chain EXP-08/09 measured. **The
two gain controls do not.** At these amplitudes the spans are 5-24 counts, so
±1 count is 4-20% and the low points sit in the noise floor; the fits are
effectively carried by the single largest amplitude. That bounds what this
experiment can claim: it can tell "responds to signal" from "does not", and it
can place a gain within a factor of ~1.3. **It cannot measure a gain**, and no
number below should be read as one.

### Ranges 0, 1, 2 — the hypothesis is refuted

All 1024 samples sit at 255 **with the generator off and after a centring pass
on a quiet input**. There is no drive to be too large. Whatever pins these rows
does so with no signal present, so it cannot be an overdrive artifact, and the
`SCOPE_CAL_NONE` tier stands — though for a better-established reason than
before.

### Range 3 — the hypothesis survives, and it is an OFFSET problem

Range 3 is not dead. Span tracks amplitude on both channels (3→62 counts on
CH1, 3→78 on CH2), and CH2's usable points imply roughly **5.6 mV/count** —
below range 4's 9.49, i.e. genuinely more sensitive, and inside the 0.4-8
mV/count family Stlkv measured. Treat that figure as an order of magnitude, not
a measurement (see §4).

What makes it unusable is not gain. Its quiet mean sits at **250.5 / 235.1**
instead of 128, so the waveform clips against the top rail — 453 and 520 of
1024 samples railed at the largest amplitude, while range 4 at the same drive
rails not one.

And EXP-07's centring table already recorded why, without anyone noticing:

| range | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CH1 DAC1 code | 2047 | 2047 | 2047 | **7** | 727 | 2047 |
| CH2 TMR13 code | 2047 | 2047 | 2047 | **3** | 827 | 2047 |

On range 3 the binary search drove **both** offset injectors to the bottom of
their 0-4095 travel — codes 7 and 3 — and still could not bring the mean to
128. The injector runs out of range. On 0/1/2 it returns 2047, the midpoint,
which is what the search reports when it sees no response at all.

## 6. Blind spots

- **This cannot measure gain**, only presence and rough magnitude — the two
  known-gain controls came back 9-26% off. The 5.6 mV/count for range 3 is
  indicative.
- **The generator floor is 12 mVpp reported.** If ranges 0-2 are sensitive
  enough that even 12 mVpp rails them, this design still could not tell that
  from "pinned". The quiet-input result is what rules that out, not the sweep.
- **Only DC-coupled, only one frontend posture.** `CLAUDE.md` records that the
  sensitive rows may need PC12/PA15 in a state we did not set here. Not varied.
- **Nothing here explains WHY 0/1/2 pin high** — open input, grounded tap,
  missing bias, or a relay that never engages are all consistent.
- **One unit.**

## 7. Conclusion

- **REFUTED for ranges 0/1/2:** they are not sensitive rows being overdriven.
  They rail at 255 with no signal at all and after a quiet-input centring pass.
- **SURVIVES for range 3:** it responds to signal, is more sensitive than range
  4, and its defect is **offset, not gain** — the vertical-offset injector hits
  the end of its travel (DAC1 code 7, TMR13 code 3) and still cannot pull the
  mean off 235-250.
- **Established, and it is the useful part:** the reason range 3 is unusable is
  now named and is potentially fixable in firmware, where "purpose unknown" was
  not. Anything that extends offset travel on the sensitive rows — a different
  injector drive, an added bias, or whatever stock does that we do not — would
  make range 3 usable.
- **NOT excluded:** that 0/1/2 are also offset failures rather than dead taps.
  A pinned rail is what an uncentrable sensitive row looks like *in the limit*,
  and our injector demonstrably runs out of travel one row over.
- **Method note worth keeping:** centring must be done on a quiet input. Doing
  it under drive is what made range 3 look identical to 0/1/2 in EXP-08.
- **Follow-up:** (1) ask what stock writes to DAC1/TMR13 on ranges 0-3 — if it
  drives them outside the span our binary search explores, that is the answer;
  (2) check whether PC12/PA15 change the picture; (3) re-run range 3 with a
  source that can hold 5-30 mVpp accurately, which the incoming generator can.
